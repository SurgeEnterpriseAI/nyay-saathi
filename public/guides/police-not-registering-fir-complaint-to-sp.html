Police not registering your FIR: complaint to the Superintendent of Police

For a serious (cognizable) offence the police must register an FIR. If the police station refuses, the law gives you two more steps, and free legal aid can help.

In danger right now? 112 · Free lawyer: 15100

Steps

  1. For a serious (cognizable) offence the police must register an FIR. You can file it at any police station; it can be transferred later ("Zero FIR").
  2. Ask for a free copy of the FIR after it is registered.
  3. If the station refuses, send your complaint in writing to the Superintendent of Police (section 173(4) of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023). Keep proof of sending. The template below does this.
  4. If that also fails, you can apply to the Magistrate (section 175(3) BNSS). Free legal aid (15100) can help you with this.
  5. For online fraud or cyber crime, call 1930 immediately and report at cybercrime.gov.in.

Questions people ask

What can I do if the police refuse to register my FIR?

Send your complaint in writing to the Superintendent of Police under section 173(4) of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023, and keep proof of sending. If that also fails, you can apply to the Magistrate under section 175(3) BNSS.

Can I file an FIR at any police station?

Yes. You can file it at any police station and it can be transferred later. This is called a Zero FIR.

Do I have to pay for a copy of the FIR?

No. Ask for a free copy after it is registered.

Who do I call for online fraud?

Call 1930 immediately and report at cybercrime.gov.in.
