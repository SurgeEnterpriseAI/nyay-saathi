Who can get a free lawyer in India? Section 12, Legal Services Authorities Act

In India, many people are entitled by law to a free lawyer, whatever their income. The list is in section 12 of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987. Women and children alone are more than half the population, yet most people have never heard of legal aid.

Who is entitled (section 12)

Anyone who has to file or defend a case is entitled to free legal services if they are:

  • Member of a Scheduled Caste or Scheduled Tribe
  • Victim of trafficking in human beings or forced labour (begar)
  • Woman
  • Child (below 18)
  • Person with a disability
  • Victim of mass disaster, ethnic violence, caste atrocity, flood, drought, earthquake or industrial disaster
  • Industrial workman
  • Person in custody (including protective home, juvenile home, psychiatric hospital)
  • A person whose yearly income is below the limit set by the State Government (or by the Central Government for cases in the Supreme Court)

If you are in one of the first eight groups, your income does not matter.

The income limit is set by each state and changes over time, so Nyay Saathi does not guess a number. Ask your District Legal Services Authority whether your family income qualifies.

What else the law says

  • The Legal Services Authority must be satisfied that you have a prima facie case to bring or defend (section 13(1)).
  • An affidavit about your income can be enough to show you are eligible, unless the Authority has reason to disbelieve it (section 13(2)).
  • Legal Services Authorities also run Lok Adalats. A Lok Adalat award is final and binding, with no appeal against it (section 21(2)). There is no court fee, and if a court case is settled there, the court fee already paid is refunded (section 21(1)).

Source: the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987, as published by NALSA at nalsa.gov.in.

How to apply

  1. Call 15100 (NALSA legal aid helpline), or visit the District Legal Services Authority at your district court.
  2. Bring an ID and any papers about your case.
  3. Say which group you belong to, or what your yearly family income is, and what your problem is. The written application below covers all three.

Questions people ask

Can a woman get a free lawyer in India regardless of income?

Yes. Section 12(c) of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987 makes every woman and every child entitled to free legal services, whatever their income.

What is the income limit for free legal aid?

Each State Government sets its own limit (the Central Government sets it for Supreme Court cases), and it changes over time. Ask your District Legal Services Authority, or call 15100, whether your yearly family income qualifies.

Are industrial workers entitled to free legal aid?

Yes. An industrial workman is one of the categories in section 12, so income does not matter.

How do I apply for a free lawyer?

Call 15100 or go to the District Legal Services Authority at your district court with an ID and your case papers. You can hand in a written application; Nyay Saathi drafts one for free.

Do I have to prove my income?

An affidavit about your income can be enough, unless the Authority has reason to disbelieve it (section 13(2)).
