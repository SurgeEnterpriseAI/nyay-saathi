Unpaid salary or wages: demand letter and what to do next

If your employer or contractor has not paid you, start with a written demand. If that does not work, a claim at the Labour Department is free, and workers can usually get free legal help.

In danger right now? 112 · Free lawyer: 15100

Steps

  1. Write down what you are owed: dates worked, daily or monthly rate, total due, and the employer or contractor name and address.
  2. Keep any proof: attendance register photos, WhatsApp messages, bank transfers, witnesses who worked with you.
  3. Send a short written demand to the employer (the template below). Keep a copy or photo.
  4. If you are not paid, file a claim at your district Labour Department office (Labour Commissioner / Assistant Labour Commissioner). Filing a claim there is free.
  5. Call 15100 for free legal aid if the employer still refuses. Workers are usually eligible for free legal help; an industrial workman is entitled under section 12 of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987 whatever their income.

Questions people ask

What can I do if my employer does not pay my salary?

Send a written demand, keep a copy, and if you are still not paid, file a claim at your district Labour Department office. Filing there is free. Call 15100 for free legal aid.

What proof should I keep?

Attendance register photos, WhatsApp messages, bank transfers, and names of witnesses who worked with you.

Can a worker get a free lawyer?

Workers are usually eligible. An industrial workman is entitled to free legal services under section 12 of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987 whatever their income. Call 15100 to check.
