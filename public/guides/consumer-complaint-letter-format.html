Consumer complaint letter format: refund, replacement or repair

Paid for something that was defective, or a service that was not delivered? Complain to the seller in writing first. If that fails, the National Consumer Helpline and the District Consumer Commission are free routes that do not need a lawyer.

In danger right now? 112 · Free lawyer: 15100

Steps

  1. Collect the bill or invoice, order screenshots, warranty card and every message with the seller.
  2. Complain to the seller in writing first and give a date to fix it (for example 15 days). The template below does this.
  3. Register a free grievance with the National Consumer Helpline: call 1915 or use consumerhelpline.gov.in.
  4. If it is still unresolved, file a complaint with the District Consumer Commission online on e-Daakhil. You can file it yourself without a lawyer. Claims up to ₹50 lakh go to the District Commission.
  5. File within 2 years of the problem arising.

[Seller / company name]
[Seller address or customer-care email]

Subject: Complaint about deficiency in goods/service and demand for remedy

Sir/Madam,

On [date of purchase] I paid ₹[amount] to you. The problem:
[Describe the defect or service failure and what you already tried]

I ask you to [refund ₹___ / replace the product / repair it] within 15 days of this letter.

If this is not done, I will file a complaint before the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, and claim compensation and costs.

[Your full name]
[Your address]
Phone: [Your phone number]
Date: [Date]

Questions people ask

Do I need a lawyer to file a consumer complaint?

No. You can file a complaint with the District Consumer Commission yourself, online on e-Daakhil.

What is the National Consumer Helpline number?

1915. You can also register a grievance at consumerhelpline.gov.in.

How long do I have to file a consumer complaint?

File within 2 years of the problem arising.

Which Consumer Commission do I go to?

Claims up to ₹50 lakh go to the District Consumer Commission.
