RTI application format: ask a government office for information

If a government office is sitting on your pension, ration card or any application, the Right to Information Act, 2005 lets you ask it for the status in writing. Here is what to do and the application Nyay Saathi drafts.

Steps

  1. Under the Right to Information Act, 2005 you can ask any public office for information, for example the status of your pension, ration card or application.
  2. Write a short RTI application (the template below). The fee is ₹10; people below the poverty line pay nothing (attach a copy of the BPL card).
  3. For central government offices you can file online at rtionline.gov.in. For state offices, many states have their own portal, or send it by post to the Public Information Officer.
  4. The office must reply within 30 days. If it does not, or the reply is wrong, file a first appeal within 30 days.

The letter Nyay Saathi drafts: RTI application (Right to Information Act, 2005)

This is the template. In the app you describe your problem, fill in what you know, and copy or download the letter. Anything you leave out stays in [brackets] for you to complete by hand before you sign. Keep a copy.

To,
The Public Information Officer,
[Name of the office / department]
[Address of the office]

Subject: Request for information under section 6(1) of the Right to Information Act, 2005

Sir/Madam,

Please provide the following information:
[1. The current status of my application no. ___ dated ___
2. The name of the officer it is pending with
3. Copies of file notings on it]

The application fee of ₹10 is paid by [postal order / court fee stamp / online receipt no. ___].

I request the information within 30 days as required by section 7(1) of the Act.

Name: [Your full name]
Address: [Your address]
Phone: [Your phone number]
Date: [Date]

Signature

Questions people ask

What is the fee for an RTI application?

₹10. People below the poverty line pay nothing if they attach a copy of their BPL card.

How long does the office have to reply to an RTI?

30 days, under section 7(1) of the Right to Information Act, 2005.

What if I get no reply or a wrong reply?

File a first appeal within 30 days.

Where do I file an RTI online?

For central government offices, at rtionline.gov.in. For state offices, many states have their own portal; otherwise send it by post to the Public Information Officer of that office.
